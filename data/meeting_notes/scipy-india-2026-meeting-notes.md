Format notes for whoever takes minutes:

* One `## YYYY-MM-DD — <title>` heading per meeting. The date must be ISO.
* `Facilitator:` and `Attendees:` are comma-separated.
* Action items are checkboxes. `owner:`, `workgroup:`, `status:` and `due:` are
  all optional — leave them out when the meeting did not decide them. The
  pipeline records a missing owner as unassigned rather than guessing one.
* An action item repeated in a later meeting is the same action item, which is
  how status moves along. Two items in the same workgroup that genuinely say the
  same thing need an `id:` to stay apart. You rarely need one.
